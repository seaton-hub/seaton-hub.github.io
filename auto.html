Why Seaton Auto

More Than a Shipper.
Your Vehicle Partner.

Wholesale Auction Access

We buy at dealer and auction wholesale prices — not retail. Your vehicle costs less before it even hits the water.

Pre-Shipment Inspection

Every vehicle is inspected and photographed before loading. You know exactly what you're getting before it sails.

Full Documentation

We handle title transfers, export permits, and Ghana customs clearance paperwork — you receive all original documents.

Insured Container Shipping

Vehicles are professionally secured in dedicated or shared containers with full marine insurance throughout transit.

Port-to-Door Delivery

We clear customs at Tema Port and coordinate final delivery to your home or compound anywhere in Ghana.

WhatsApp Updates

Track your vehicle through every milestone — from auction purchase to container loading to port arrival — all via WhatsApp.
