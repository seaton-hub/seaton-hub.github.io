The Process

Simple Steps,
Zero Hassle

We've stripped out the complexity so you can focus on what matters — getting your goods home.

01

Tell Us What You Need

Send us a message on WhatsApp or fill out the quote form. Let us know what you want to ship — type, size, quantity.

02

We Source & Price It

We find the best wholesale price for your item wherever it is — or you can source your own. We quote the full cost, no surprises.

03

We Pick Up & Pack

04

Delivered in Ghana

Your items clear customs, and we coordinate delivery to your door or designated location in Ghana. Done.

Who We Are

We Are Seaton Logistics —
Built for Our Community

Seaton Logistics was born out of a simple frustration: Ghanaians abroad had no reliable, affordable way to ship goods back home. Dealers charged too much. Middlemen cut corners. And nobody really understood the community they were serving.

We changed that. As a Ghanaian-owned and operated company, we know both sides of this journey — the streets of Accra and every major shipping port in the world. We source vehicles, appliances, and equipment at wholesale prices that others can't access, and we ship them safely to Ghana with full transparency at every step.

We're not just a logistics company. We're your partner, your neighbour, and your connection home — no matter where in the world you are.

Integrity First No hidden fees. No surprises. Ever.
