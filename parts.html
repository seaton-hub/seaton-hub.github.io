What We Supply

Keep It Running.
Always.

Downtime costs money. Seaton Parts sources genuine OEM and trusted aftermarket components across all makes and models — so your vehicles and equipment never stay idle for long.

Car & Truck Parts

Engine components, brake parts, suspension, transmission, body panels, and electrical parts for Toyota, Honda, Ford, Nissan, Hyundai, and all popular brands sold in Ghana.

Heavy Machinery Parts

Undercarriage components, hydraulic cylinders, wear parts, track pads, and engine overhaul kits for Caterpillar, Komatsu, Volvo, and XCMG heavy equipment.

Engine Oils & Lubricants

Castrol, Mobil 1, Shell Helix, Valvoline, and Motul engine oils — petrol and diesel grades, all viscosities, from 1-litre bottles to 200-litre industrial drums for fleet and workshop use.

Industrial Lubricants & Grease

Gear oils, hydraulic fluids, transmission fluids, multi-purpose grease, and specialty lubricants for construction equipment, agricultural machinery, and industrial plant.

Filters & Fluids

OEM and OE-quality oil filters, air filters, fuel filters, cabin filters, and coolant — single units or bulk packs for fleet workshops doing scheduled maintenance at volume.

Batteries & Electrical Parts

Optima, Exide, and Bosch car and truck batteries, alternators, starters, sensors, and lighting — sourced in bulk for fleet operators and distributed to workshops across Ghana.

Why Seaton Parts

Genuine Parts.
Guaranteed.

OEM-Verified Sourcing

We source directly from authorised OEM distributors and certified aftermarket suppliers — every part carries a genuine part number and traceable supply chain.

Compatibility Guaranteed

You provide the vehicle make, model, year, and VIN — we verify the correct part number before ordering. No guesswork, no wrong fitment, no wasted money.

Quality Testing

Critical parts — brake calipers, engine mounts, hydraulic seals — are inspected and tested before shipping. We will not send a part we wouldn't put on our own vehicle.

Bulk Fleet Pricing

Fleet operators and workshops ordering in volume receive tiered pricing — the higher the quantity, the lower the per-unit cost. We accommodate standing orders and blanket contracts.

Complete Documentation

Every consignment ships with a full packing list, customs invoice, and certificate of origin — making Ghana clearance straightforward and your records audit-ready.

Fast Emergency Sourcing

Equipment offline? Send us the part number via WhatsApp and we'll confirm availability within hours and fast-track a shipment to minimise your downtime.
