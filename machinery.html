What We Import

Built for the
Biggest Jobs.

From road construction to earthmoving, Seaton Machinery sources the most trusted brands — Caterpillar, Komatsu, Volvo, XCMG — and delivers them ready to work on your site.

Motor Graders

Caterpillar, Komatsu and XCMG graders for road construction and surface levelling — available in various blade widths and horsepower ratings for any project scale.

Asphalt Pavers

Vogele, Dynapac, and XCMG asphalt pavers for road surfacing projects — wheeled and tracked options sourced at wholesale from European and Asian equipment dealers.

Rollers & Compactors

Single-drum, tandem, and pneumatic tyre rollers from Bomag, Dynapac, and Hamm — essential for compacting asphalt and subgrade on road construction projects.

Excavators

Mini to large hydraulic excavators — Caterpillar, Komatsu, Hyundai, and Volvo — for earthmoving, trenching, and foundation work. New and low-hour used units available.

Bulldozers & Crawlers

Track-type tractors for heavy earthmoving, clearing, and reclamation — Caterpillar D6 through D9 and Komatsu equivalents sourced from North America and Japan.

Cranes & Lifting Equipment

Mobile cranes, tower cranes, and rough-terrain forklifts for construction and industrial sites — sourced to spec and shipped with all lifting certifications and documentation.

Why Seaton Machinery

From Factory Floor
To Your Site.

Manufacturer-Direct Pricing

We source directly from manufacturers, certified dealers, and government-surplus auctions — cutting out intermediaries so you pay less for the same equipment.

Pre-Shipment Inspection

Every machine is physically inspected, engine-tested, and photographed before loading. You receive a full condition report before payment is finalised.

Complete Documentation

We prepare all export titles, ECOWAS certificates, Ghana customs entries, and DVLA documentation — your equipment is road and site-legal from day one.

Insured Ocean Freight

Heavy machinery is secured in flat-rack or open-top containers with heavy-lift rigging and comprehensive marine insurance covering full replacement value.

Port-to-Site Delivery

We manage customs clearance at Tema Port and coordinate low-loader transport directly to your construction site or equipment yard anywhere in Ghana.

Project Consultation

Not sure which spec you need? Our team reviews your project requirements and recommends the right horsepower, blade configuration, and brand — saving you costly mistakes.
