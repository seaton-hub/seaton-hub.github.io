Simple Process

How Renting
Works.

Getting heavy equipment on your site is straightforward. From first contact to machine delivery, we handle every step.

01

Choose Equipment

Browse our fleet above and select the machine that matches your project's requirements. WhatsApp or call us to confirm the spec you need.

02

Confirm & Book

We confirm availability, agree on rental period and pricing, and lock in your booking. A deposit secures the machine for your project dates.

03

Delivered to Site

We load and transport the machine directly to your project site using our low-loader fleet. Setup and handover included — ready to work from day one.

04

Return or Extend

At the end of your rental period, we collect the machine — or simply call us to extend. Flexible terms, no penalties for well-maintained returns.
