What We Import

Land, Water,
and Air.

Whether you crave the open trail, open water, or the open sky — Seaton Recreation sources the finest powersports and water-sports equipment from worldwide, delivered to Ghana.

ATVs & Quad Bikes

Yamaha Raptor, Honda Foreman, Can-Am Outlander, Polaris Sportsman — sport and utility ATVs for recreational riding, farm use, and off-road trails across Ghana.

Jet Skis & PWCs

Sea-Doo, Yamaha WaveRunner, and Kawasaki Jet Ski personal watercraft — two and three-seater models sourced from North America and ideal for Ghana's coastal and lake waters.

Flyboards & Water Jets

Zapata Flyboard, Jetpack Aviation, and iFly water-jet packs — spectacular hydrojet equipment for coastal resorts, waterparks, and personal use on Ghana's shores.

Dirt Bikes & Motocross

KTM, Husqvarna, Honda CRF, Yamaha YZ — motocross and enduro dirt bikes for competitive racing and trail riding, sourced from USA and European dealers.

Golf Carts & Utility Vehicles

Club Car, EZ-GO, and Yamaha electric and gas golf carts for resorts, gated communities, warehouses, and golf courses — street-legal and off-road utility models available.

Parts & Accessories

Helmets, protective gear, spare parts, and performance upgrades for everything we sell — sourced alongside your main order to keep you equipped and ready to ride.

Why Seaton Recreation

Ride Harder.
Pay Smarter.

Premium Global Brands

We work exclusively with recognised powersports brands — Sea-Doo, Yamaha, Can-Am, KTM, Polaris — so you get the real thing, not a knockoff.

Sport Certification

Every powersport unit comes with its original title, serial number, and manufacturer certificate — essential for registration and insurance in Ghana.

Specialised Crating

ATVs, jet skis, and dirt bikes are drained, secured on custom wooden cradles, and enclosed in purpose-built crates to survive ocean transit without a scratch.

Insured Ocean Shipping

Full marine insurance covers your powersports equipment from the seller's warehouse to your hands — with real-time WhatsApp updates at every milestone.

Customs Expertise

We handle the Ghana customs classification and duties for recreational vehicles — a specialist area many freight agents get wrong. We've done it hundreds of times.

After-Sale Support

We remain your point of contact after delivery — connecting you with local technicians, sourcing spare parts, and answering questions about your equipment long after it arrives.
