Why Seaton Appliance

Genuine Products.
Zero Compromise.

100% Genuine Products

We source exclusively from authorised distributors and licensed retailers. Every item is brand new, sealed in original packaging — never refurbished or grey-market.

Warranty Documentation

You receive the original manufacturer warranty card and receipt with every purchase — valid proof for any warranty claims within the standard warranty period.

Professional Packing

Appliances are bubble-wrapped, foam-protected, and crated to survive ocean transit intact. Fragile items get individual shock-resistant packaging.

Home Delivery

From Tema Port straight to your front door. We handle customs clearance and coordinate delivery across Accra, Kumasi, and beyond — white-glove service available on request.
