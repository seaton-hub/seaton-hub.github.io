What We Import

Every Trade.
Every Tool.

From the auto workshop to the fabrication shop, Seaton Tools brings you the professional-grade equipment that gets the job done — at prices that make sense for your business.

Air Compressors

Ingersoll Rand, DeWalt, Makita, and Campbell Hausfeld — portable and stationary compressors from 6-gallon workshop units to 80-gallon industrial tanks for body shops and garages.

Welding Equipment

Lincoln Electric, Miller, Hobart, and ESAB MIG, TIG, and stick welders — from entry-level workshop sets to multi-process industrial units for fabrication and structural work.

Power Tools

DeWalt, Makita, Milwaukee, and Bosch drills, grinders, circular saws, jigsaws, and rotary tools — cordless and corded options for carpentry, metalwork, and construction.

Workshop Machinery

Hydraulic car lifts, engine hoists, press brakes, lathes, and drill presses — workshop infrastructure equipment for auto garages, fabrication shops, and industrial facilities.

Hand Tool Sets

Snap-on, Stanley, Craftsman, and Wera complete socket sets, combination wrenches, screwdriver sets, and toolboxes — professional-grade sets for mechanics and tradespeople.

Safety & PPE Equipment

Welding helmets, grinding shields, safety boots, gloves, harnesses, and workshop first aid kits — sourced alongside your tools so your team stays protected from day one.

Why Seaton Tools

Trade Prices.
No Middleman.

Wholesale Trade Pricing

We buy tools and equipment at industrial distributor prices — not retail. A compressor that costs four times more locally comes to you at a fraction of that through Seaton Tools.

Brand Authenticity

Every tool carries the original brand serial number and purchase receipt — no clones, no rebranded generics. You get exactly the brand performance you're paying for.

Workshop Delivery

Heavy equipment is delivered directly to your workshop or site — we coordinate unloading logistics so your team can get started immediately without organising separate transport.

Complete Documentation

All customs entries, import permits, and brand certificates are prepared and provided — giving you clean paperwork for your business records and any future warranty claims.

Bulk Order Discounts

Equipping a workshop or industrial site? Order in bulk and benefit from tiered pricing — the more you order, the lower your per-unit cost. Ideal for large-scale fit-outs.

Expert Sourcing

Tell us the brand and model or describe the job — our team will identify the right spec, compare models, and recommend the best value-for-money option for your application.
